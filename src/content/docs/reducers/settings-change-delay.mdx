**Settings Change Delay** delays a settings change that makes a restriction weaker. The current restriction stays active during the delay.

## How the delay works

- Curbox saves a weaker change as a pending change.
- The current settings stay active during the delay.
- Curbox applies the pending change when the delay ends.
- You can cancel a pending change before Curbox applies it.
- A stricter change applies immediately and removes a weaker pending change for the same feature.
- A shorter delay is a weaker change. The current delay applies before Curbox changes it.
- Turning off the delay is a weaker change. The current delay applies before Curbox turns it off.

You can select a delay from 10 minutes through 2 days.

## Examples

| Change | Application time |
|---|---|
| Increase an app limit from 30 minutes to 2 hours | After the delay |
| Remove an app from a blocked group | After the delay |
| Turn off Keywords And Websites | After the delay |
| Add an app to a blocked group | Immediately |
| Change the delay from 2 days to none | After the current 2-day delay |

## Require Tamper Protection to be off

Turn on **Require tamper protection off** to add a second condition. A weaker change stays pending after its delay ends while Tamper Protection is active.

Turn off [Tamper Protection](../tamper-protection/) to let Curbox apply the pending change.

<Aside type="note">
This option requires both conditions. The delay must end, and Tamper Protection must be off.
</Aside>
