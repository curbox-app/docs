**Block Short Form Content** stops you from scrolling through reels and shorts once you hit a limit you set. You can cap by time, by video count, or both — and control exactly when and where the block applies.


## Supported Apps

Block Short Form Content works in these apps:

- Instagram (Reels)
- Instagram lite 
- YouTube (Shorts)
- Facebook (Reels) (experimental)
- Tiktok

Curbox doesn't endorse any mod apps or illegal activities. These have been added by community members to the project.
- MyInsta
- Youtube ReVanced
- Morphe

btw if you're kinda tech savvy, you can easily add support for a new mod. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/nethical6/curbox/)


## How It Works

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Active Schedule** | Defines the time window(s) when the block is active. Short-form content is blocked during this period. |
| **Usage Limit** | *(Optional)* Sets how long you can watch short-form videos during the active schedule before it blocks. Leave empty to block for the entire schedule window. |
| **Configure Reel Count** | *(Optional)* Sets how many reels or shorts you can scroll through before the block triggers. |
| **Allowed Apps** | Apps excluded from this configuration — short-form content in these apps will not be blocked. |

<Aside type="note">
The reel count and time are combined across all supported apps. If you set a limit of 20 videos, watching 10 Reels on Instagram and 10 Shorts on YouTube counts as 20 total.
</Aside>


## Setting It Up

<Steps>
1. **Open Short-Form Video Content**
   Tap **Reducers**, then tap **Short-Form Video Content**.

2. **Toggle the main switch on**
   The switch is at the top right of the screen.

3. **Set the Active Schedule**
   Tap **Active Schedule** to define when the block should be active — which hours and days of the week.

4. **Set a Usage Limit** (optional)
   Tap **Usage Limit** to cap total watch time during the active schedule. Leave empty to block for the entire window.

5. **Configure Reel Count** (optional)
   Tap **Configure Reel Count** to set how many reels or shorts can be scrolled before the block triggers.

6. **Set Allowed Apps** (optional)
   Tap **Allowed Apps** and select any apps that should be ignored by this configuration.

7. **Configure the warning screen** (optional)
   Tap **Configure Warning Screen** to choose what happens when the block triggers. See [Unlock Challenges](BASE_URL/unlock-challenges/overview) for the full guide.
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
A single schedule block cannot cross midnight. For an overnight block like 11 PM to 2 AM, create two separate blocks: 11 PM to 12 AM, and 12 AM to 2 AM.
</Aside>
