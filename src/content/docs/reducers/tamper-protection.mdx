**Tamper Protection** makes common bypass actions more difficult. It uses the Android device administrator permission and the Curbox accessibility service.

## Actions that Curbox monitors

While Tamper Protection is active, Curbox monitors attempts to:

- Uninstall Curbox
- Remove its device administrator permission
- Turn off its accessibility service
- Open Android settings that can turn off its protection

When Curbox detects one of these actions, it closes the applicable screen or returns to the previous screen.

## Unlock methods

| Method | Function |
|---|---|
| **Password** | Turns off Tamper Protection after you enter the correct password |
| **Timed unlock** | Turns off Tamper Protection after the selected number of days; you cannot cancel this timer |
| **One-day wait** | Turns off Tamper Protection after one day; you can cancel the request during the wait |

Select an unlock method that you can complete when you must change a necessary Android setting.

## Interaction with Settings Change Delay

[Settings Change Delay](../settings-change-delay/) can require Tamper Protection to be off. If this option is active, a weaker settings change has two conditions:

- Its delay must end.
- Tamper Protection must be off.

Curbox does not apply the pending change until both conditions are true.

## Limitations

<Aside type="caution">
Tamper Protection adds resistance, but it is not an absolute security control. A factory reset or another system-level action can remove the protection.
</Aside>
