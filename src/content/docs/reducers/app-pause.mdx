**App Pause** applies a schedule and an optional usage limit to a group of apps. When a block starts, Curbox shows the configured warning screen.

Focus Mode is for a session that you start manually. App Pause is for a group that follows a repeated schedule.

## App Pause controls

Each App Pause group has these controls:

| Control | Function |
|---|---|
| **Active Schedule** | Sets the days and times when the group applies |
| **Usage Limit** | Sets the permitted app-use duration during the active schedule |

If **Usage Limit** is empty, Curbox blocks the apps for the complete active schedule. If you set a limit, Curbox blocks the apps after you use that duration.

Example: A group is active from 9:00 PM to 7:00 AM. An empty limit blocks the apps for that complete period. A 30-minute limit permits 30 minutes of use during that period.

## Create an App Pause group

<Steps>
1. **Open Apps**
   Tap **Reducers**. Then, tap **Apps**.

2. **Create a group**
   Tap the **+** button. Curbox opens **Create App Group**.

3. **Enter a name**
   Enter a short name in **Name**.

4. **Select apps**
   Tap **Select Apps**. Select one or more apps.

5. **Set the active schedule**
   Tap **Active Schedule**. Select the days and times when the group applies.

6. **Set a usage limit (optional)**
   Tap **Usage Limit**. Enter the permitted use duration.

7. **Configure the warning screen (optional)**
   Tap **Configure Warning Screen**. Configure the action that is necessary to continue. Read [Unlock Challenges](../../unlock-challenges/overview/) for all options.

8. **Save the group**
   Tap **Done**. Curbox activates the group.
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Open the menu in the app selector to copy an app list from another reducer group.
</Aside>

## Configure an all-day or overnight schedule

Set the start and end times to 12:00 AM for an all-day schedule.

Curbox automatically splits a time range that crosses midnight. For example, Curbox stores 11:00 PM to 2:00 AM as two adjacent ranges.

## Manage groups

The **Apps** screen shows the name, number of apps, and active schedule for each group.

Turn off the switch on a group card to pause the group. Turn on the switch to activate the group again.

## Use the same app in multiple groups

You can put the same app in multiple groups. The active schedules for that app must not overlap.

If two schedules overlap, Curbox shows a warning. Change one schedule before you activate both groups.

### Example: daytime limit and overnight block

| Group | Active schedule | Usage limit |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Daytime | 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM | 45 minutes |
| Instagram Night Block | 10:00 PM to 9:00 AM | Empty |

The daytime group permits 45 minutes of use. The night group blocks Instagram for the complete overnight period.

### Example: weekday and weekend limits

| Group | Active schedule | Usage limit |
|---|---|---|
| Social Weekday Limit | Monday through Friday, all day | 30 minutes |
| Social Weekend Limit | Saturday and Sunday, all day | 90 minutes |

Each group has an independent usage counter.
