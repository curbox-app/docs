Curbox is an Android app that helps you reduce your screen time. You can set limits, block distractions, and monitor your device use.

## Main areas

Curbox has three main areas:

- **Usage** shows your app use, website use, and short-form video use.
- **Focus** blocks selected apps and websites during a timed session.
- **Reducers** contains scheduled limits and prompts. Reducers can block apps, block content, or make an app less distracting.

## How Curbox works

Curbox uses Android accessibility services to identify the app and content on the screen. When a configured rule applies, Curbox starts the action for that rule.

For example, Curbox can show a warning screen when you open a blocked app. Your warning-screen configuration controls how you can continue.

Curbox stores its data on your device. Curbox does not request the Android internet permission.
