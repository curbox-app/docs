**Short-Form Video Content** blocks short-form videos when a configured condition is true. You can use a schedule, a watch-time limit, a video-count limit, or a combination.

## Supported apps

Curbox includes support for these official apps:

- Instagram and Instagram Lite for Reels
- YouTube for Shorts
- Facebook for Reels (experimental)
- TikTok

Community-maintained rules also support these modified apps:

- MyInsta
- YouTube ReVanced
- Morphe

Support for a modified app can stop after an app update. Report a problem or contribute a rule in the [Curbox repository](https://github.com/nethical6/curbox).

## Controls

| Control | Function |
|---|---|
| **Active Schedule** | Sets the days and times when the configuration applies |
| **Usage Limit** | Sets the permitted watch duration during the active schedule |
| **Configure Reel Count** | Sets the permitted number of short-form videos |
| **Allowed Apps** | Excludes selected apps from this configuration |

If **Usage Limit** is empty, Curbox blocks short-form videos for the complete active schedule.

<Aside type="note">
The time and video counters include all supported apps. For example, 10 Instagram Reels and 10 YouTube Shorts equal 20 videos.
</Aside>

## Configure a short-form video block

<Steps>
1. **Open Short-Form Video Content**
   Tap **Reducers**. Then, tap **Short-Form Video Content**.

2. **Turn on the reducer**
   Turn on the switch in the upper-right corner.

3. **Set the active schedule**
   Tap **Active Schedule**. Select the days and times when the block applies.

4. **Set a watch-time limit (optional)**
   Tap **Usage Limit**. Enter the permitted watch duration.

5. **Set a video limit (optional)**
   Tap **Configure Reel Count**. Enter the permitted number of videos.

6. **Exclude apps (optional)**
   Tap **Allowed Apps**. Select each app that Curbox must exclude.

7. **Configure the warning screen (optional)**
   Tap **Configure Warning Screen**. Configure the action that is necessary to continue. Read [Unlock Challenges](../../unlock-challenges/overview/) for all options.
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
A single time range cannot cross midnight. Use two ranges for an overnight schedule.
</Aside>

For 11:00 PM to 2:00 AM, use 11:00 PM to 12:00 AM and 12:00 AM to 2:00 AM.
