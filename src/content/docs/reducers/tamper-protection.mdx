**Tamper protection** makes Curbox difficult to remove or disable during moments of temptation.

When enabled, Curbox uses Android's device administrator permission and watches for attempts to bypass its protections. If it detects one of these attempts, it leaves the screen or sends you back.


## What It Watches For

- Uninstalling Curbox.
- Removing its administrator permission.
- Turning off the Curbox accessibility service.
- Opening system screens that could disable its protection.


## Unlocking Tamper Protection

Tamper protection can be unlocked in three ways:

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Password** | Protection turns off immediately after the correct password is entered. |
| **Timed unlock** | Protection turns off automatically after the chosen number of days. Once started, the wait cannot be stopped. |
| **One-day wait** | Protection turns off after one day, but the request can be cancelled during that wait. |


## Interaction with Settings Change Delay

While tamper protection is active, pending weaker settings changes may also be held back if the **Require tamper protection off** option is enabled in [Settings Change Delay](../reducers/settings-change-delay). This means a weaker change will not apply even after its timer runs out until tamper protection is turned off.


## Limitations

<Aside type="caution">
Tamper protection is a strong friction layer, not an absolute security guarantee. A device owner, factory reset, or other system-level control can still override it.
</Aside>
