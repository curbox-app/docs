**Settings change delay** is a protection against impulsive weakening of your limits.

When enabled, any change that makes a restriction weaker does not apply right away. The stronger rules stay in place while the wait runs out, giving you a buffer between a weak moment and an actual loss of protection.


## How It Works

- Changes that make a restriction weaker are saved as pending.
- The current, stronger rules keep working during the wait.
- The new settings apply only after the selected delay.
- Available delays range from **10 minutes** to **2 days**.
- You can cancel a pending change before it takes effect.
- If you make a stricter change to the same feature, it applies immediately and removes the weaker pending change.
- Turning off the delay or choosing a shorter delay is itself treated as a weaker change, so that change must also wait.


## Examples

| Action | When it applies |
|---|---|
| Increasing an app limit from 30 minutes to 2 hours | After the delay |
| Removing blocked apps | After the delay |
| Disabling the keyword blocker | After the delay |
| Adding a stricter block | Immediately |
| Changing the delay from 2 days to "None" | After the current delay |


## Require Tamper Protection Off

There is an optional **Require tamper protection off** setting. When enabled, weaker changes remain blocked even after their timer ends — they will not apply until [Tamper Protection](../reducers/tamper-protection) is turned off first.

<Aside type="tip">
This option pairs well with a long delay. The combination means that weakening a setting requires both waiting out the delay **and** going through the tamper protection unlock process.
</Aside>
